# User Story 5: Display the Battle Screen

## Title
Display the battle screen

## User Story
As a player,  
I want to see the battle screen,  
so that I can understand the current state of the fight and interact with the game.

## Description
When a battle begins, the game should display a battle screen that shows the player and the current monster.  
The screen should present the most important combat information, such as health points, available cards, and the active battle state.  
This gives the player a clear overview of the fight and prepares the next game actions.

## Acceptance Criteria
- The battle screen is shown when a battle starts.
- The player information is displayed on the battle screen.
- The current monster is displayed on the battle screen.
- The health points of the player and the monster are visible.
- The player’s available cards are visible.
- The battle screen reflects the current game state.
- The battle screen is ready for card actions during combat.
